## Bounded Question

Which active Pi packages, extensions, and tracked `pi-personal-config` capabilities may be
considered for Omicron Code, who owns them, where do they overlap, and which material is
optional, development-only, machine-local, or forbidden?

## Evidence Contract

Bind exact safe source identities and classify capability surfaces without copying or
revealing credentials, tokens, OAuth material, trust records, sessions, caches, private
audits, or machine-specific values. Inspection must leave local Pi and
`pi-personal-config` unchanged.
